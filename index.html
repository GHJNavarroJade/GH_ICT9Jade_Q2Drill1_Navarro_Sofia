Basic Strategy
  • Touch Limit

    A maximum of 3 hits can only be applied to each team to return the ball.

  • Rotation

    Players rotate for each members to be able to serve.

  • Boundary Line

    When serving a player must not cross the line or it will be out. Although, during a rally players may go out the line to chase the ball.

  • Knee pads

    Must be prepared and it is a need. Because it includes diving to save the ball.

  • Footwear

    A proper and stable shoes plays a significant role to volleyball players. This helps them run and jump properly.

  • Team players

    Each team is required to have a maximum of 6 players to be able to play in courts.

  • Three-Touch Rule

    This strategy includes having to bump, set, and spike the ball.

  • Targetting

    This can have an advantage to volleyball players. Because, once they see a weakness in one of the oponents team. They may target that individual based on their weakness.

  • Defense

    For Liberos, it is important for them to chase the ball and save it and blockers will block the ball when the opnent tries spiking.
